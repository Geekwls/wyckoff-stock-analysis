# Wyckoff Analysis Examples

## Example 1: Identifying Accumulation Phase

### Stock: XYZ Corp (Hypothetical)

**Step 1: Identify the Context**
- Previous trend: Downtrend from $100 to $40 over 6 months
- Current price: Trading sideways between $38-$45 for 8 weeks

**Step 2: Locate Key Wyckoff Events**

```
$100 ├─── Previous Uptrend
      │
      │ (Decline begins)
      ▼
$50  │
     │
     ▼
$45  ╞════════════════════════════════════════
     │  AR      ST    Spring  SOS   LPS
$40  ───╱──────╱──────╱──────╱──────╱─────
     ╱  ╲    ╱  ╲   ╱      ╱     ╱
$38 ╱    ╲  ╱    ╲─╱      ╱     ╱
    │     CL       ╲     ╱     ╱
$35 ┼────────────────────╱─────╱
    │   PS              ╲   ╱
$30 ┼─────────────────────╱─╱
    │
    └─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯
    8   6   4   2   0   2   4  6  8  Weeks
```

**Step 3: Volume Analysis**
- Week 8 (PS): Volume spike to 3M shares (highest in 3 months)
- Week 6 (CL): Extreme volume of 5M shares, panic selling
- Week 5 (AR): High volume of 2.5M shares, quick rebound
- Week 4 (ST): Lower volume of 800K shares, supply being absorbed
- Week 2 (Spring): Moderate volume of 1.2M shares, false breakdown
- Week 0 (SOS): Strong volume of 2.8M shares, breakout confirmation

**Step 4: Phase Identification**
- **Phase A**: Complete (PS, CL, AR, ST identified)
- **Phase B**: Complete (8 weeks of consolidation)
- **Phase C**: Complete (Spring occurred and successfully tested)
- **Phase D**: In progress (SOS confirmed, looking for LPS)

**Step 5: Trading Strategy**

**Entry**: Wait for LPS (Last Point of Support)
- Target entry zone: $42-$43
- Pullback to test the SOS breakout
- Expected volume: 1-1.5M shares (lower than SOS)

**Stop Loss**: $37.50 (below the Spring low)
- Risk: $5 per share
- Risk per trade (2% of $50K account): $1,000
- Position size: 200 shares

**Target**: $52 (Cause = $45-$38 = $7; $45 + $7 = $52)
- Potential gain: $9 per share
- Risk/Reward ratio: 9:5 = 1.8:1

**Step 6: Execution Checklist**
- [ ] SOS confirmed with volume > 2M
- [ ] LPS pullback occurs on declining volume
- [ ] Price holds above $41
- [ ] Overall market not in strong downtrend
- [ ] Sector showing relative strength

## Example 2: Identifying Distribution Phase

### Stock: ABC Inc (Hypothetical)

**Step 1: Identify the Context**
- Previous trend: Uptrend from $30 to $120 over 12 months
- Current price: Trading sideways between $105-$118 for 10 weeks

**Step 2: Locate Key Wyckoff Events**

```
$120 ╞══════════════════════════════════════════
     │      LPSY  SOW
$118 ─────╱──────╱──────────────────────────────
     ╱   ╱      ╱
$115 ╱   ╱      ╱  AR      ST    Upthrust
    ╱   ╱      ╱──╱──────╱──────╱─────
$105───────────╱  ╲    ╱  ╲   ╱
    │         ╱    ╲  ╱    ╲─╱
$100┼────────╱      CL       ╲
    │  PSY  ╱               ╲
$95 ┼─────╱────────────────────────────────────
    │
$90 ─┴──────────────────────────────────────────
    └─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯└─╯
    10  8  6  4  2  0   2   4  6  8  10 Weeks
```

**Step 3: Volume Analysis**
- Week 10 (PSY): Volume spike to 4M shares, first significant supply
- Week 8 (CL): Extreme volume of 6M shares, buying climax
- Week 7 (AR): High volume of 3M shares, quick decline
- Week 6 (ST): Lower volume of 1.2M shares, demand being absorbed
- Week 2 (Upthrust): Moderate volume of 1.5M shares, false breakout
- Week 0 (SOW): Strong volume of 3.5M shares, breakdown confirmation

**Step 4: Phase Identification**
- **Phase A**: Complete (PSY, CL, AR, ST identified)
- **Phase B**: Complete (10 weeks of topping formation)
- **Phase C**: Complete (Upthrust occurred and failed)
- **Phase D**: In progress (SOW confirmed, looking for LPSY)

**Step 5: Trading Strategy (Short Position)**

**Entry**: Wait for LPSY (Last Point of Supply)
- Target entry zone: $108-$110
- Rally to test the SOW breakdown
- Expected volume: 1-1.8M shares (lower than SOW)

**Stop Loss**: $120 (above the Upthrust high)
- Risk: $10 per share
- Risk per trade (2% of $50K account): $1,000
- Position size: 100 shares (short)

**Target**: $98 (Cause = $118-$108 = $10; $108 - $10 = $98)
- Potential gain: $10 per share
- Risk/Reward ratio: 10:10 = 1:1 (consider waiting for better setup)

**Alternative**: If Risk/Reward < 1.5:1, skip this trade

**Step 6: Execution Checklist**
- [ ] SOW confirmed with volume > 3M
- [ ] LPSY rally occurs on declining volume
- [ ] Price fails below $115
- [ ] Overall market showing weakness
- [ ] Sector showing relative weakness

## Example 3: Re-accumulation in Uptrend

### Context: Stock in ongoing uptrend

**Pattern Characteristics:**
- Occurs during Phase E (Markup) of the larger cycle
- Shorter duration than major accumulation (3-6 weeks vs 8-12+ weeks)
- Shallow trading range relative to the uptrend
- Quick resolution with continuation of uptrend

**Trading Approach:**
1. Identify the uptrend is intact (higher highs and higher lows)
2. Wait for LPS within the re-accumulation range
3. Enter long position with tight stop below range
4. Target measured move or next resistance level

**Example Structure:**
```
$80 ───╱  New highs after re-accumulation
    ╱ ╱
$70 ╱╱
   ╱╱
$60╱└────────────────────────────────
   │  LPS  (Entry)
$55┼──╱─────────────────────────────
  ╱
$50╱────────────────────────────────
  │
$45┴────────────────────────────────
```

## Example 4: Failed Pattern Analysis

### When Wyckoff Patterns Fail

**Important:** Not every trading range results in the expected continuation

**Signs of Failed Accumulation:**
1. SOS occurs but fails to accelerate
2. LPS breaks down through support
3. Volume fails to confirm breakout
4. Multiple failed springs
5. Overall market turns bearish

**Signs of Failed Distribution:**
1. SOW occurs but finds support
2. LPSY breaks through resistance
3. Volume fails to confirm breakdown
4. Multiple failed upthrusts
5. Overall market turns bullish

**Risk Management for Failed Patterns:**
- Adhere strictly to stop losses
- Don't average losing positions
- Reassess analysis if pattern fails
- Be willing to reverse position if market clearly shows it
